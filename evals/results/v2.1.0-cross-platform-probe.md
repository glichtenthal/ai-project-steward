## Limitations

The two installations contained byte-identical `SKILL.md` instructions, but platform routers, companion instructions, tools, and models differ. Activation telemetry was not retained uniformly for every probe, so the ordinary-request control is judged from the observed response rather than a platform-level routing trace. The reversible-change fixtures were isolated local copies and did not test a live application. This result does not establish deterministic enforcement or identical behavior across every model, platform, or future run.
