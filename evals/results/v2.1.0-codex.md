## Limitations

Positive cases explicitly loaded the candidate SKILL.md, so they test steward behavior rather than autonomous routing. The two negative controls were unforced, but Codex CLI does not expose skill-selection telemetry; their routing judgments are inferred from the direct, non-governance responses. Cases ran in isolated synthetic workspaces with no real accounts, production data, deployment, or cross-platform testing. Temporary gpt-5.6-sol capacity required gpt-5.6-terra for cases 7–9. Case 9 was first observed with an incomplete failure-path inventory, the candidate completion wording was clarified, and a separately retained Terra rerun was reviewed. These results are observations under the named conditions, not guarantees across models or platforms.
